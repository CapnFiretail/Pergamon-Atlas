What is Pergamon Atlas?

Pergamon Atlas is my attempt to build one place where useful tools, fun ideas, helpful content, and random creations can all live together.

Why I’m making it

I wanted a project that could hold everything I’m interested in — not just one niche, but an expandable system where I can keep building without hitting a wall.

Trust the process

Iron Man built it in a cave. I’m building this in physics class, late at night, and somewhere between ambition and sleep deprivation.
