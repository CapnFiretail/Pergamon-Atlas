The Road

Atlas Runner is a simple endless runner. The explorer keeps moving. Your only job is to jump over whatever comes. The pace climbs steadily — survive as long as you can.

How to Play

  • Press Space or tap to jump.
  • Clear every relic and pillar.
  • Speed increases over time.
  • Your best run saves in the browser.
