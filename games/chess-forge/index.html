Chess Forge

Classic local chess on one screen. Standard movement rules are included, plus check, checkmate, and stalemate detection.

Controls

Click a piece to see legal moves. Then click a highlighted square to move.

Special rules: automatic queen promotion is enabled. Castling and en passant are not yet implemented.

Turn

White to move.

Game State

Match in progress.

Blue = selected piece.
Green = legal move.
Red = legal capture.
