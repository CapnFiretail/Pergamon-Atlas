Chess Forge

Classic chess on one screen. Play locally with a friend or challenge the computer. Full standard movement, check, checkmate, and stalemate detection included.

Mode

Controls

Click a piece to see its legal moves, then click a highlighted square to move.

Auto-promotion to Queen is enabled. Castling and en passant are not yet implemented.

Turn

White to move.

Status

Match in progress.

Gold — selected piece
Green — legal move
Red — legal capture
