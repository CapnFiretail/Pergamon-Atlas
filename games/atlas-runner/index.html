The Premise

Atlas Runner is built as a quick arcade game for Pergamon Atlas. It is intentionally simple, clean, and replayable: survive, improve, and keep the lane changes crisp.

How to Play

  • Dodge every obstacle.
  • Your score rises as you survive longer.
  • The road speeds up over time.
  • Your best score saves in the browser.
