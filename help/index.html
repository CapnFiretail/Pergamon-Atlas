The Atlas Guide

Pergamon Atlas is an infinitely expanding library; withing it, everything that has been and ever will be. As of yet, Archivists have only uncovered and catalogged a few games, tools, and systems in the Library.

Use the navigation systems, catalogs, and search functions to traverse the Atlas and uncover indexed artifacts!

Atlas Codes and Addresses

Every artifact within the Atlas possesses an assigned Atlas Address and internal Atlas code.

These identifiers are generated through deterministic indexing systems tied to the underlying structure of the Atlas itself.

The Pergamon Constant

The Pergamon Constant is an undocumented mathematical foundation believed to influence Atlas generation, indexing behavior, and organization.

Its true nature remains uncertain.

Feedback & Support

Report damaged artifacts, indexing failures, artifact anomalies, or unstable chambers to the Atlas Maintenance Division.

User feedback assists with Atlas stability, refinement, and future expansion of the Atlas.
