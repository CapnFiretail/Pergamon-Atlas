About BMI

BMI uses height and weight to estimate whether someone falls into a general weight category. It is useful as a quick screening tool, but it does not directly measure body fat, muscle mass, athletic build, or overall health.

  • Underweight: below 18.5
  • Normal: 18.5 to 24.9
  • Overweight: 25.0 to 29.9
  • Obese: 30.0 and above
