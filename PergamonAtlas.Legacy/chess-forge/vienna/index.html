About the Vienna Game

The Vienna Game arises after 1.e4 e5 2.Nc3, a move that shores up the center and prepares an aggressive kingside expansion with f4. Unlike the Italian or Ruy Lopez, the Vienna sidesteps a mountain of theory while retaining sharp, fighting positions.

White's plans are flexible: the Vienna Gambit (f4), the quiet Vienna with d3, and the sharp Frankenstein-Dracula Variation all branch from this single knight move. Understanding the common ideas unlocks all of them.
